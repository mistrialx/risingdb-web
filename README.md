# Rising DB

An unofficial, offline wiki and toolkit for **Destiny: Rising**, built straight from the game's own data.

**Open it:** https://mistrialx.github.io/risingdb-web/

It covers every character, weapon, perk, mod, artifact and set, plus Exotic Resonance picks, activities, drop rates, enemies, draws, Shadowshaper Duels cards and more. It also has tools: a build planner, a perk roll planner, a pity tracker, side-by-side compare, and saved favourites.

No account, no ads, no tracking. After the first visit it works with no internet connection.

---

## Install on iPhone or iPad

1. Open **https://mistrialx.github.io/risingdb-web/** in **Safari**.
   It has to be Safari. Chrome and other browsers on iOS can't add it to the home screen as an app.
2. Tap the **Share** button: the square with an arrow, at the bottom of the screen on iPhone or the top on iPad.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Leave the name as **Rising DB** and tap **Add**.
5. Open **Rising DB** from your home screen **once while you're online**. That saves everything the app needs to your phone.

It now opens full-screen like a normal app and works offline. Images are saved the first time you see them, so a page you've opened before shows its pictures offline too.

**Updating:** when the game updates, the app is updated here. Open it while online and it picks up the new version by itself. If it still shows old data, close it fully (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again.

## Install on Android

**Chrome:** open the link, tap **⋮** (top right), then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

## Use on PC or Mac

Open the link in any browser. Chrome and Edge show an **Install** icon in the address bar if you want it as an app.

---

## Your saved builds and favourites

Builds, favourites and the pity tracker are saved **on your device only**. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

- To move them to another device or keep a copy, open **Saved** and tap **Save a backup file**. On iPhone it goes to the Files app. On the other device, use **Restore from a backup file**. Restoring adds to what's there and never deletes anything.
- On iPhone, use the home-screen app rather than a Safari tab. iOS can clear the saved data of websites you haven't visited in a while, but not of apps added to the home screen.
- Private Browsing doesn't keep anything after you close it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| No "Add to Home Screen" option | You're not in Safari. Copy the link into Safari. |
| Blank page or missing data offline | Open it once while online, and leave it open for a few seconds so it can finish saving. |
| Old data after a game update | Close the app fully and reopen it while online. |
| Some items have no picture | About 2% of the game's icons aren't in the game files, so those entries have no image. |
